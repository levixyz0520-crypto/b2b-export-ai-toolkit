# Customer Research

## Purpose

Build a source-backed buyer brief that separates verified facts, reasonable hypotheses, and unknowns.

## Inputs

- Company name, country, website, or public profile
- Product category and target market
- Optional research constraints and approved sources

## Outputs

- Company and buyer-type summary
- Evidence table with source URL and retrieval date
- Product-fit hypotheses labeled as hypotheses
- Missing information, risks, and next research steps

## Workflow

1. Sanitize the input and minimize personal data.
2. Confirm entity identity before combining sources.
3. Collect public evidence relevant to importing, distribution, retail, projects, and product fit.
4. Record a URL and retrieval date for each material claim.
5. Separate facts, hypotheses, and unknowns.
6. Recommend proportionate next steps and human checks.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent companies, contacts, activity, scale, certifications, or intent.
- Do not bypass access controls or terms of service.
- Do not collect sensitive personal data or private contact lists.
- Treat absence of evidence as unknown, not negative evidence.
- Require human review before qualification or outreach.

## Example

Input: `Northstar Demo Furnishings, Exampleland; compressed sofas` (fictional).

Output: a brief marking the example domain as fictional, listing public checks to perform, and leaving import history unknown until sourced.
